## Limits

V1.1 analyzes one explicitly selected current source. Document and selected-source
retrieval completeness remain `not_assessed`; support assessment remains
`not_performed`. Native coverage flags are limited observations. Logical
read-only SQLite snapshots can maintain ordinary WAL sidecars and do not imply
byte-identical database-family files.

Citation/reference and currentness validation do not prove scientific factual
correctness or semantic entailment. Multi-paper synthesis, automatic
supported/inferred/not_reported/conflicting/unreadable decisions, OCR execution,
UI/API, conversation memory, general production readiness and corpus-wide
performance guarantees remain outside this validated scope.
